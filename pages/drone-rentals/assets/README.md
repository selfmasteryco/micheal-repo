# Assets — Drone Rentals

Drop drone images here and update `src` attributes in `index.html` to point to the files.

Recommended files:
- `stealth-bird-4k.webp` — Stealth Bird 4K Carbon product shot
- `holy-stone-hs360s.webp` — Holy Stone HS360S product shot
- `dji-mini-4k.webp` — DJI Mini 4K product shot
- `ruko-f11gim2.webp` — Ruko F11GIM2 product shot

Use `.webp` format for photos. Recommended size: 400x300px at 2x (800x600px source).
